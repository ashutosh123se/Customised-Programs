DURATION

Program Duration — You Decide

Best for: Keynotes, Ice-breakers & Large-scale Assemblies

Ideal for annual events, college orientation programs, and high-impact motivational sessions. A compact, fast-paced injection of insight designed to challenge paradigms and trigger initial curiosity. Includes core psychological frameworks and Sajan Shah's high-octane stage presentations.

Best for: Focused Skill Seminars & Executive Briefings

Provides 3 to 4 hours of interactive workshop time. Suitable for targeting a singular organizational pain point, such as team motivation, confidence-building, or basic productivity principles. Features practical exercises and initial neuroscience-based habit-building strategies.

Best for: Deep-Dive Workshops & Mid-level Corporate Training

A comprehensive 6 to 8-hour transformation container. Allows for in-depth diagnostic exercises, personal analysis, interactive cohort simulations, and customized action blueprints. Ideal for corporate soft-skills building, mental wellness intensives, or parent-teacher alignments.

Best for: Immersive Retreats & Leadership Bootcamps

Spans over 48 hours of learning. Deeply engages participants in simulation labs, communication workshops, and structural analysis. Best suited for management development programs, executive retreats, and senior leadership alignment campaigns.

Best for: Long-term Behavioural Architecture & Institutional Shift

Delivered in recurring modular formats over weeks or months. Features systematic checkpoint audits, individual feedback analysis, digital micro-learnings, and custom reinforcement guides. This system creates verified, long-term neural reprogramming and measurable results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can request a customised program? +
Schools, colleges, coaching institutes, corporates, organisations, parents' groups, leadership teams, and any community with a shared goal.
Can programs be delivered online? +
Yes. We offer online, offline, and hybrid formats depending on your needs and location.
How long does it take to design a customised program? +
After your initial consultation, most programs are designed within 3–7 working days depending on complexity.
What is the minimum number of participants? +
We generally recommend a minimum of 20–25 participants for optimal engagement. Contact us for smaller groups.
Are programs available in regional languages? +
Yes. Programs can be delivered in Hindi, Gujarati, English, and select regional languages based on your requirement.

Absolutely. We work with institutions on annual event calendars, term-wise programs, and long-term engagement series.

Pricing is customised based on audience size, program type, duration, and format. Contact us for a tailored quote.
